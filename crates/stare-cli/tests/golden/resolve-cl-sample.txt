The question is whether a hauler's written estimate is an offer. We have held that a residence, not a mailing address, is the test of domicile. D'Elia & Marks Co. v. Lyon, 31 A.2d 647 (D.C. Mun. Ct. App. 1943).

The order of the Superior Court is affirmed.

[dissent — Wecht]

I respectfully dissent. An estimate invites an offer; it does not make one.
